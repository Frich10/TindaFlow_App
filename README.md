## 📱 Introducing TindaFlow

**TindaFlow** is a mobile application, built for **Android**, that helps small business owners monitor **sales, inventory, operating expenses, GCash transactions, customer debt (utang), and estimated profit** through a centralized dashboard.

Many small business owners, such as sari-sari store and tindahan owners, still rely on notebooks, text messages, and memory to run their business. TindaFlow brings everything into one app so owners can see how their business is really doing, at a glance.

---

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 📊 **Centralized Dashboard** | A single view of sales, expenses, and estimated profit. |
| 💰 **Sales Monitoring** | Record and review daily sales. |
| 📦 **Inventory Management** | Keep track of stock levels and products. |
| 🧾 **Operating Expenses** | Log business costs to understand true earnings. |
| 📱 **GCash Transactions** | Track cash-in and cash-out transactions and fees. |
| 📒 **Customer Debt (Utang)** | Record who owes what and monitor payments. |
| 📈 **Estimated Profit** | Automatically estimate profit from sales and expenses. |
